# Business UI Brief

Record verified product facts and selected constraints. Omit sections that do
not apply; keep unknowns explicit rather than inventing a complete application.

## User And Task

- Role and access scope.
- Task to complete, relevant product objects and success evidence.
- Requested screen or flow, existing behavior to preserve and change boundary.

## Screen And Data

- Relevant list, detail, edit, navigation or drill-down behavior.
- Required columns, filters, saved views and bulk actions, when applicable.
- Field validation, dirty state, save progress, failure and recovery behavior.
- Real data source, KPI definition, units, time period and refresh/stale policy.
- Relevant loading, empty, error and permission states; demonstration data label.

## Design Constraints

- Existing design system or verified screen reference and its location.
- Supported devices, layouts and useful information density.
- Keyboard, focus and accessibility requirements relevant to the task.
- Selected original author provider, required native tools and known exposure.

## Verification

- Rendered artifacts for the supported layouts.
- Affected task, interaction and data checks with their actual results.
- Unresolved product facts, runtime gaps and decisions still needed.
